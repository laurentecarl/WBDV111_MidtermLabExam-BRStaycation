About BR Staycation

BR Staycation is your cozy and relaxing place designed to provide guests with comfort, convenience, and a truly restful experience. We offer stylish yet affordable accommodations where you can unwind, recharge, and escape the stress of everyday life. Choose from our three uniquely designed rooms — Room 1, Room 2, and Room 3 — each offering a warm, inviting atmosphere perfect for relaxation. Each room comfortably accommodates 1-4 guests, making it ideal for couples, families, or small groups. For guests beyond the standard room capacity, additional person charges may apply. We also feature a Garden Rooftop with a relaxing city view, perfect for unwinding and enjoying the scenery. For families, we offer a playground and kiddie pool pass (ages 1–12), making BR Staycation a fun and comfortable escape for both adults and kids. Spacious suites, beautiful views, and family-friendly amenities all come together to create a memorable holiday experience.

Our Mission

Our mission is to give every guest a warm and memorable experience by offering clean, well-designed, and relaxing spaces that feel just like home.
